## Highlights & Projects

### Fedora Project (Package Maintainer)
Active package maintainer for **Fedora Linux** and **EPEL (Extra Packages for Enterprise Linux)**, specifically focusing on scientific C++ libraries, distributed computation, and analytics tooling.
- [Fedora Contributor Wiki](https://fedoraproject.org/wiki/User:Denisarnaud)
- [Maintained Packages on Bodhi & PkgDB](https://admin.fedoraproject.org/pkgdb/users/packages/denisarnaud)

### C++ Libraries for Air Travel & Operations Research
Architecture and development of modular open-source C++ libraries modeling airline business rules, reservation systems, and schedule planning:
- **AirTSP / StdAir**: Models for airline distribution and inventory systems.
- **AirSched / AirInv / SimFQT**: Routing engines, revenue simulation, and fare quotation.
- Available on [GitHub (@da115115)](https://github.com/da115115).

### Open Standards Advocacy
Member and contributor to the **FFII (Foundation for a Free Information Infrastructure)**, defending software innovation and open standards in Europe.
